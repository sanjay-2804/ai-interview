Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the platform.

Are the questions based on real placement papers?

Yes. All 540+ aptitude questions and 310+ coding problems are modeled directly on quantitative, logical, verbal, and technical assessments from leading IT and product recruitment drives.

Does my progress save if I close the browser?

Yes. All practice sessions, answered questions, test scores, speaking attempts, bookmarks, and career goals are permanently stored in the backend database.

How does the Communication Lab analyze my voice?

Your browser captures real audio using the standard Web Audio/MediaRecorder API. Speech is transcribed and evaluated against a transparent 5-pillar rubric measuring cadence (WPM), filler words, relevance, clarity, and grammatical structure.
