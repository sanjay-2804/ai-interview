Placement Preparation FAQs

Why are answers locked immediately upon submission?

Real campus recruitment tests (TCS iON, AMCAT, CoCubes, eLitmus) enforce immutable answer submissions. InterviewAI preserves the submitted state so students analyze their actual thought process rather than re-guessing.

How do company recruitment cutoffs work?

Most mass recruiters enforce a 60-65% sectional cutoff across Quantitative, Logical, and Coding sections. Tier-1 product firms (Google, Amazon) require 80-85%+ with zero failing test cases.

How does the ATS Resume Builder score my resume?

The ATS engine scans your listed projects, technical skill keywords (OOP, SQL, REST APIs), and academic CGPA against typical employer screening parser criteria.
