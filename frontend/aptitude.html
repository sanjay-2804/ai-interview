Placement Formula & Shortcut Cheatsheet

Time & Work

• If A does work in X days, 1-day work = 1/X.
• If A and B work together, time = (A × B) / (A + B).
• Work = Rate × Time. Efficiency is inversely proportional to time.

Speed, Distance & Trains

• Speed = Distance / Time. To convert km/h to m/s: multiply by 5/18.
• Relative Speed (opposite directions) = S1 + S2.
• Relative Speed (same direction) = |S1 - S2|.
• Train passing platform: Distance = Length of train + Length of platform.

Profit & Loss

• Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100.
• Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100.
• SP = CP × (100 + Gain%) / 100.
• Two items sold at same SP, one at +x% and other at -x%: overall always a loss of (x/10)².

Permutations & Probability

• nPr = n! / (n - r)! (Arrangement matters).
• nCr = n! / [r! × (n - r)!] (Selection without order).
• P(E) = Favourable outcomes / Total outcomes.
• P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B).
